## Synthetic-media disclosure

Some assets are AI-generated. Disclose synthetic narration and label illustrative/reconstructed imagery; never imply it is authentic case evidence. Confirm the platform's current disclosure requirements before publication.

## Required checks

- Verify case facts, allegations, names, quotations, dates, and current legal status.
- Confirm permissions, licenses, and all required credits.
- Watch the complete rendered MP4; check captions, pacing, voice, and music.
- Do not publish a REVIEW DRAFT or VISUAL NEEDED placeholder as a finished episode.
