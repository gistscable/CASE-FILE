# Publication handoff — Inspection workflow demo — SYNTHETIC MEDIA

This is a producer checklist, not a guarantee that the video is ready to publish.

## Chapter draft

```text
0:00 HOOK
0:03 CHAPTER 1
```

Chapter draft needs adjustment before use as automatic YouTube chapters: provide at least three chapters, starting at 0:00, with each section at least ten seconds long.

## Required checks

- Verify case facts, allegations, names, quotations, dates, and current legal status.
- Confirm permissions, licenses, and all required credits.
- Watch the complete rendered MP4; check captions, pacing, voice, and music.
- Do not publish a REVIEW DRAFT or VISUAL NEEDED placeholder as a finished episode.

See sources.md, preflight.md, and project.json for the source/rights trail and the exact export snapshot.
