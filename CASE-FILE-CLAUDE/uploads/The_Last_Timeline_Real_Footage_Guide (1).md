**Jevario Buie / Jahmir Moore**  •  **Research checked:** October 30, 2026  •  **Use with:** `The_Last_Timeline_Narration.txt`

**This replaces the previous stock-footage plan.** The links below are to actual case reporting, police releases, real surveillance, case photographs, and courtroom reporting—not actors, reenactments, or generic stock.

## What is available—and what is not

- **April surveillance:** [Tampa Police’s actual three-person recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a0NS9AsqdeA), filmed before Jevario was killed and released May 9, 2024. It does **not** show the shooting. I have not established that it is either of the separately described 3:33 or 3:36 recordings.
- **Crime-scene vicinity:** [10 Tampa Bay’s April report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z0GGKSasvIg&t=42s) includes railroad imagery while reporting this case. [WFLA’s April report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKuxl_fYYrQ&t=41s) includes uniformed figures in a wooded setting. **The exact location and date of those individual shots have not been independently confirmed.** Ask the stations before calling either shot the exact crime scene or the April 17 response. No verified video of the shooting itself was located.
- **December escape:** [Tampa Police’s six-second business-camera clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5pMeJcySADY) is real, but it is **escape surveillance**, not footage of the killing.
- **News, police and court:** Direct links below include the actual arrest report, reward and recapture briefings, no-bond report, verdict update and sentencing coverage. The December no-bond report is subject to FOX’s restrictive reuse policy; the 10 Tampa Bay verdict and sentencing segments are alternative case footage for later chapters, not substitutes for that particular hearing.

**Review method:** I checked source descriptions/reporting and visually inspected sampled YouTube storyboard frames, generally at 1–5 second intervals, plus the two direct photographs. For the reference follow-up, I sampled the two compilations at 10-second intervals; sampled the original WFLA and 10 Tampa Bay vigil reports at two-second intervals; and sampled the actual WFTS funeral and FOX trial videos at five-second intervals, with closer one-second samples around the suggested windows. I did not watch the videos continuously or audition their audio. Time ranges below are **approximate preview positions**, not frame-accurate in/out points: open each link, review the moving clip and any audio in context, and make final cuts in your editor.

**How to edit:** Keep your existing narration intact. By default, mute source audio and put these visuals under the exact phrases below. A cleared news or police sound bite can instead go *between* narration paragraphs if you want the reference video’s broadcast feel; check the complete surrounding remarks and avoid distress audio. Date-label material recorded or released later than the event being narrated. Use only footage you have permission to reuse or for which your lawyer has advised on the specific use; no source here is represented as already licensed.

**Standing boundaries:** No reenactment, graphic material, distress audio or images, grief spectacle, private phone contents, or identification of the unnamed third teenager. Chapter 3 must contain **no residential-property images**. No detailed escape route or home address. A brief plain text warning or source/date label is an editorial overlay, not fake footage.

Ask **publicrecords@hillsboroughsao.gov**, **(813) 274-1938**, whether a non-graphic, publicly releasable version exists and who controls reuse; see its [public-records page](https://hillsboroughsao.gov/contact-us/public-records/). Do not request or use graphic exhibits or private phone contents. Records may be withheld, redacted or subject to fees. The office notes that emails sent to it may be public records.

For each request, specify **monetized YouTube documentary**, worldwide online distribution, intended term, approximate seconds, whether you want video and/or audio, promotional clips, and any crop/reframe. Save the written approval and required credit. Copyright ownership can be layered; a broadcaster’s permission may not cover an embedded family photo or private CCTV. Credit is useful but is not itself permission. Fair use is fact-specific, and there is no automatic safe number of seconds: see the [U.S. Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html). No one can promise zero claims or lawsuits.
